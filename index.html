Who Are We?

Vulpera Ecological Collective is the world's first Deaf-owned wildlife conservation initiative whose goal is to bring nature education to millions of children around the world virtually and in schools, as well as to maintain numerous projects around the world pertaining to cleanup, research, and conservation to restore the balance of our natural world.

The Maned Wolf

The Maned Wolf (𝘊𝘩𝘳𝘺𝘴𝘰𝘤𝘺𝘰𝘯 𝘣𝘳𝘢𝘤𝘩𝘺𝘶𝘳𝘶𝘴) is one of South America’s most unique canids. Despite its name and fox-like appearance, it is neither a true wolf nor a fox and is the only species in its genus. Its long legs allow it to move easily through the tall grasslands of Brazil, Paraguay, Bolivia, and Argentina. Unlike many predators, the maned wolf is an omnivore and plays an important ecological role by eating fruits and dispersing seeds across the ecosystem. Habitat loss and road collisions are some of the biggest threats to this fascinating species. Protecting grasslands helps ensure that animals like the maned wolf can continue playing their role in maintaining healthy ecosystems.
