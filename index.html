Who Are We?

Vulpera Ecological Collective is the world's first Deaf-owned wildlife conservation initiative whose goal is to bring nature education to millions of children around the world virtually and in schools, as well as to maintain numerous projects around the world pertaining to cleanup, research, and conservation to restore the balance of our natural world.

Conservation Status

See that circle beside the animal name? That indicates the animal's conservation status. The list below explains what each color means:

  • ● Least Concern - The species is widespread and abundant.
  • ● Near Threatened - The species is close to qualifying for a threatened category.
  • ● Vulnerable - The species faces a high risk of extinction in the wild.
  • ● Endangered - The species faces a very high risk of extinction in the wild.
  • ● Critically Endangered - The species faces an extremely high risk of extinction in the wild.
  • ● Extinct in the Wild - The species is no longer found in the wild.
  • ● Extinct - The species has no documented living individuals remaining.
  • ● Data Deficient - There is insufficient information to assess the species' risk of extinction.
