Opening the Doors to Accessibility

As a Deaf-led conservation group, we are opening the pathways to disability awareness and accessibility. This is why we provide entirely visual education resources, inclusive environmental education, and accessible wildlife learning materials. We want everyone to feel included!

As a Deaf person myself, I know what it feels like to be belittled and pushed down, and I would never wish this upon anyone. We all deserve to be recognized for who we are and what we CAN do. Unfortunately, very few conservation organizations highlight accessibility, and many disabled people who are interested are left stumped. With Vulpera, I have made everything, our team included, disability-friendly, so no matter what hardships you're facing out in the real world, you'll always be included and recognized here!
