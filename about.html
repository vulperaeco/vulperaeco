About Us

The Vulpera Ecological Collective is an independent wildlife conservation and education initiative dedicated to protecting biodiversity and inspiring the next generation to appreciate the natural world.

Our goal is not only to support wildlife conservation efforts, but also to help people, especially children, learn about the animals and plants that share our planet. Through educational programs and outreach, we hope to spark curiosity and encourage future conservationists.

In the future, we aim to support wildlife sanctuaries that recreate natural environments where animals can live with minimal human interference. These sanctuaries would focus on animal wellbeing while also contributing to conservation research and education.

Vulpera also plans to host annual virtual educational programs focused on wildlife, ecosystems, and environmental science so that students around the world can learn about nature and conservation.

Our Founder

Vulpera Ecological Collective was founded by Soren Saal, a Deaf wildlife enthusiast and author with a vision of making conservation and environmental education accessible to everyone.
